Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Texas Ranch Meat collects, uses, stores and shares information when you visit our website, create a customer profile or place an order.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect your phone number, name, delivery address, ZIP code, order contents, order history, payment method selection, payment status, customer support communications, and basic technical information needed to operate and secure the website. We do not ask you to enter full payment card numbers into this website.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and maintain customer profiles, process and manage orders, coordinate delivery, communicate about order status, provide customer support, maintain inventory and payment records, prevent misuse, secure the service and comply with legal obligations.

3. Phone numbers and verification

Phone numbers are currently collected as customer contact and account-matching information. Until the WhatsApp verification feature is activated, a phone number is not verified proof of identity. We will not treat knowledge of a phone number alone as authorization to disclose another customer's profile or order information. When WhatsApp verification is introduced, we will use it to confirm control of a number before enabling account recovery or verification-dependent communications.

4. Cookies, sessions and service providers

The application uses browser storage/session mechanisms needed to keep a customer signed in. We use Supabase to provide database, authentication and application backend services. These providers process information on our behalf to operate the service. Their handling of data is also subject to their own terms and privacy documentation.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell customer personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers who help operate the website, process orders or support delivery; when required by law or valid legal process; to protect customers, the business or the public; or as part of a business transfer, subject to applicable law. We limit information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the purpose.

6. Data security and retention

We use access controls and database permissions intended to restrict customer records to authorized accounts and administrators. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain profile and order information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain business records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. We may retain limited records after account closure where required.

7. Your choices and requests

You may request correction of profile information or ask questions about your information by contacting us. Requests to delete information will be evaluated subject to order, tax, accounting, security and other legal retention requirements. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Do not send passwords, payment card details or security codes by phone or email.

8. Children's privacy

The ordering service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted information may contact us.

9. Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Privacy questions or requests: 949-295-4650.
